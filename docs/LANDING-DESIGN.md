# TypeScribe site direction

## Product posture

TypeScribe is a working transcript desk for secretaries, executive assistants, researchers, transcriptionists, and anyone responsible for turning a recording into a clean document somebody else can use.

The main workflow is practical and time-sensitive:

1. open local media
2. run transcription when needed
3. clean timed speaker rows against the audio
4. shape the same transcript into readable Cara paragraphs
5. mark a range and send the required format

The public site should speak to the person doing that cleanup, not to an abstract software buyer.

## Chosen direction: the transcript desk

The visual metaphor is a secretary's working desk rendered as a 1970s office-supply catalogue after dark or on warm paper.

- **Stage:** a user-selectable near-black or warm-cream grain field over a subtle 36-pixel grid with no glow, interrupted by thin edge-to-edge rules and a pink, yellow, green, and cyan signal spine.
- **Header:** one bounded navigation island shared by TypeScribe and TranscriptBench. Every link reaches a real page or external destination; real Lucide SVGs mark theme, menu, download, and directional controls; the icon toggle persists the chosen site theme.
- **Hero:** the exact “THE free TRANSCRIPT dESK.” lockup uses Bricolage, a Caveat marker-script word over a painted stroke, letter-by-letter signal colors, and an irregular desk line crossed by the lower rule.
- **People and objects:** original transparent cartoon cutouts represent secretaries, assistants, researchers, and transcriptionists. Headphones, recorder, typewriter, folders, highlighter, and tape reels sit directly on the page without generated image cards.
- **Type:** Bricolage carries the hero and short chapter statements; Caveat adds handwritten marks; Hanken Grotesk carries prose; IBM Plex Mono carries timing, status, cost, and shortcuts.
- **Landing structure:** preserve the typographic hero and real Windows download, red capability ribbon, and shared illustrated footer. The selected Vestris flow now adds a broad real-app preview, a compact transcript-to-document/export explorer, and a human-control/privacy close. One original paper environment links the compositions. The preview switches between real Rows/Cara screenshots; mobile crops keep the transcript readable. The explorer uses only a labelled synthetic public-demo excerpt, with actual app formatter output and local sample downloads. Elson's removed journey, manifesto, gallery, tool grid, workflow, promise, benchmark teaser, and install/build components stay retired. Benchmark and release pages remain independent.
- **Benchmark structure:** the same island, grain, and grid lead into the interactive leaderboard, a forest five-step methodology brief with a distinct audit note, an icon-led provider source field, pink benchmark notes, and a return to the product. Methodology documents keep the same deep green in either site theme.
- **Footer:** the inspected Breaver Studios composition is adapted to warm cream and deep green. A headphone-wearing transcript sheet leans over the upper boundary, between an analog tape reel and transcript pages. Real navigation, a Windows download CTA, repository/docs/issues utilities, and route-specific metadata precede the full-width Patriot Bold wordmark. The wordmark uses Elson's local Patriot font as a bundled WOFF2, with Behalf Studio / Hoang Le / Republish attribution and its OFL notice. Transparent illustration cutouts and a join colour taken from the preceding section replace the fixed cream field. Mobile follows the reference's centred stack. Elson's later sketch specifies a flatter headband, angled earcups and loose ribbon bends. The Patriot wordmark retains its responsive width but crops the lower 30% of its capitals at the footer edge; its home link keeps a 44px minimum target. Later screenshot feedback adds thin reel rims, winding rings, a hub and fasteners while keeping its cutouts transparent. The existing green footer base now paints over the lower paper and hands at the marked edge. Duplicate paper banks are removed from the closing section only.

## Motion contract

Motion explains physical or product state:

- the four-color signal begins below the navigation island, terminates at the hero rule, and scrolls away with the hero instead of following the sticky header
- transparent office tools and character cutouts move at different rates while scrolling
- capability copy runs on a continuous office ribbon
- screenshot tabs change the selected real Rows/Cara preview with click or keyboard navigation
- the transcript explorer changes its timed rows, Cara reading text, or range/format output on explicit intent
- body sections use ordinary scrolling and bounded reveals, with no pinned chapter, autoplay, fake playback or progress bar
- desktop wheel scrolling is eased by one native-window Lenis session; touch and nested scroll areas stay native, and reduced motion disables the driver completely
- route/Back/Forward scroll positions, delayed hash destinations, keyboard intent and menu locking cancel old inertia
- the decorative paper environment is static and keeps its reading area clear
- all reveal and ambient motion collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`

Complete reference and local experience recordings live in the ignored `.coord/` research and QA folders.

## Product and privacy boundaries

- The Windows installer, release, repository, and license links are real.
- Editing and the bundled demo work without an API key.
- New transcription sends selected media to Deepgram using the user's key.
- The public benchmark JSON contains only allowlisted aggregate fields.
- Pre-grading scores must remain `null`.
- The landing build rejects unapproved JSON, local filesystem paths, and audio/video file types before copying the public tree.
- Private interview audio/text, raw provider responses, credentials, annotations, paths, and participant detail stay out of the public artifact.
- The labelled seven-row homepage sample is allowlisted synthetic public-demo text only. It reuses unchanged app formatters; its downloads are generated locally on click. No audio/video assets or new API endpoint are added.
- The Vestris implementation has source/interaction/asset tests and build checks. Direct desktop/mobile browser inspection remains a separate pending gate while the configured Zyra gateway is unavailable.
